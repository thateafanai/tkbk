Privacy Policy of Apatani Biisi Kheta

Effective Date: April 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Apatani Biisi Kheta" application ("App"), published by the Apatani Baptist Association, Ziro ("Publisher", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Not Collected by Us):

  • User Preferences: Theme, Font Size, Font Family, Keep Screen Awake setting.
  • Favorite Songs: A list of song numbers you mark as favorite.

This data remains entirely on your device within the App's local storage (shared_preferences). We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this data. It can typically be cleared by clearing the App's data in your device settings.

Information Processed by Third-Party Services (When You Initiate Action):

  • WhatsApp: Launching WhatsApp via the Feedback screen or the Comment feature uses the url_launcher package. Comments are sent to a designated WhatsApp number managed by the Apatani Baptist Association. Your interaction is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We do not collect or store data from this interaction.
  • Sharing: Using the Share button uses the share_plus package to invoke your device's native sharing function. The data shared depends on the app you choose, governed by that app's privacy policy (e.g., WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram). We do not collect or store data from this interaction.

Technical & Usage Information (Processed by Firebase):

We utilize Google's Firebase services (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Cloud Messaging):

  • Crashlytics: Crash reports including device state information, unique application instance identifiers, and stack traces related to crashes.
  • Analytics: Usage data (screens visited, session duration, feature usage), device identifiers, general location, and device info.
  • Cloud Messaging (FCM): Device-specific tokens, notification interaction data, and device information. Notifications may include images hosted on Firebase Storage.

Data collected by Firebase is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase-specific terms.

How We Use Information

  • Locally stored settings/favorites are used only for App functionality on your device.
  • Information from WhatsApp/Sharing is only used to initiate those actions.
  • Crashlytics data is used solely for diagnosing and fixing bugs.
  • Analytics data is used in aggregated or pseudonymous form to improve the App.
  • FCM data is used to send push notifications about new content or updates.

Data Storage and Security

  • User preferences and favorites are stored only locally on your device.
  • Data collected by Firebase services is stored and processed by Google according to their security practices.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell or share your locally stored preferences or favorites.
  • Firebase data is processed by Google as described in their policies. We access aggregated/anonymized forms only.
  • Interactions with third-party apps (WhatsApp, etc.) are subject to those third parties' policies.
  • Push notifications may include images hosted on Firebase Storage, but no personal data is shared.

User Choices and Control

  • Clear locally stored data via your device’s system settings.
  • Limit ad tracking or device identifiers through your device’s settings.
  • Control push notifications through your device’s notification settings.

Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child may have provided data, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date." Please review periodically for updates.
